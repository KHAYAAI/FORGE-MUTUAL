A bank its members own.
Built, not yet licensed.

Forge Mutual is a proposed South African mutual bank for technology companies and the people who build them: rand and dollar accounts, cross-border payments at one disclosed price, and team approvals, owned by its depositor-members. This document sets out everything: the model, how it makes money, whether it is sustainable, what the regulator's own forms taught us, what is built, what is drafted and what remains before it can open on a sponsor bank and apply for its own licence.
