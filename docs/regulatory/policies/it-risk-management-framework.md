**Purpose.** Applies Joint Standard 1 of 2023 (IT governance and risk management). The standard has not been read in full for this register; verify every paragraph.

## Governance

1. A dedicated IT risk function accountable to the board (JS1-03), led by the Head of IT Risk reporting to the CRO.
2. The board approves the IT strategy (`it-strategy.md`) and this framework.

## Areas

1. Change management: every change reviewed by a second person and passing the CI gate; the Change Advisory Board for production.
2. IT asset management: `docs/regulatory/assets.csv`.
3. Third-party IT risk: `provider-due-diligence-procedure.md`.
4. Resilience: `business-continuity-and-disaster-recovery-policy.md`.
5. Security: `cyber-strategy.md`, `access-control-policy.md`, `key-management-policy.md`, `secure-development-policy.md`, `secure-configuration-standard.md`.
6. Service management: `it-service-management.md`; projects: `it-project-framework.md`.

## Licence submission

1. The licence application needs a written discussion of IT systems and material outsourcing (LIC-10); it is assembled from the documents above and `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`.
