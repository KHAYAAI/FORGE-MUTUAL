# Cyber awareness and training plan

Register id: JS2-10 (Joint Standard 2 of 2024: awareness training at least annually; board training).

- **All staff and contractors:** security awareness on joining (before access to production) and at least yearly. Topics: phishing, passwords and MFA, handling restricted data, reporting an incident at once, clean desk and devices.
- **Engineers:** secure coding and the secure-coding policy; yearly. Anyone with production access also does incident-response and recovery practice.
- **Board and executives:** cyber briefing at least yearly, covering the cyber strategy, incidents, and their accountability under Joint Standard 2.
- **Records:** who, what, when, pass mark. Kept by the compliance lead (not yet appointed).
- **Phishing tests:** PROPOSED twice a year; results used to teach, not punish.
- **Measure:** share of people with current training. PROPOSED target 100% before launch.
