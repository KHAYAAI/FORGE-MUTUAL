**Purpose.** Sets out how the board of Forge Mutual is made up, what it alone decides, and how it holds management to account.

## Composition

1. PROPOSED: at least 7 directors; a majority non-executive; at least 4 independent non-executives (R05, R29 to R32).
2. At least two executive directors who are employees: the CEO (Tebogo Mvelase, proposed) and the CFO (VACANT) (MR-03).
3. The chair is an independent non-executive and is never the CEO.
4. Every director passes the Prudential Authority's fit-and-proper assessment (BA 020 / DI 020) before taking the seat; see `fit-and-proper-procedure.md`.
5. For a mutual bank: depositor-members elect directors at the general meeting under the articles (LIC-07).

## Matters reserved for the board

1. Strategy, business plan and budget.
2. The risk management framework and the risk appetite statement, at least yearly.
3. Every policy listed as board-approved in `docs/governance/POLICY_FRAMEWORK.md`.
4. Capital plan, ICAAP and ILAAP, recovery plan, dividends.
5. Appointment and removal of the CEO, CFO, CRO, CCO, MLRO, CISO and head of internal audit.
6. Material outsourcing, offshoring and cloud arrangements.
7. Licence applications, new legal entities, and any product that needs a licence decision (`pkg/licence`).
8. The annual directors' report to the Registrar on internal controls (MR-02).

## Duties of directors

1. Act in good faith and in the bank's interest (Companies Act s.76); declare conflicts before each meeting.
2. Understand the bank's risks well enough to challenge management; take induction and yearly training.
3. Meet control functions (CRO, MLRO, CCO, head of internal audit) without management present at least once a year.

## Meetings

1. At least quarterly. Quorum: a majority, including a majority of independent non-executives.
2. Papers circulated 7 days ahead. Minutes record challenge and dissent, not only decisions.
3. Resolutions in writing are allowed between meetings for urgent matters, ratified at the next meeting.

## Evaluation

1. Yearly self-evaluation; independent external evaluation every 3 years.
