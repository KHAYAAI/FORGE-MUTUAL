**Purpose.** Makes sure the financial statements and regulatory returns are complete and right, and that directors can report on it each year.

## Controls

1. Daily: the ledger trial balance balances (TigerBeetle debits equal credits) and reconciles to partner and sponsor statements.
2. Monthly: management accounts from the ledger; suspense aged and cleared; journals over R100 000 (PROPOSED) approved by the Financial Controller.
3. Every return is prepared by regulatory reporting, reviewed by the Financial Controller, certified by the CFO, and reconciled to the ledger before submission.

## Directors' report

1. Yearly the board reports to the Registrar on the effectiveness of these controls (MR-02), supported by internal audit's testing.
