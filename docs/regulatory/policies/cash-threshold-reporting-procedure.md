**Purpose.** Reports cash transactions above the prescribed threshold to the FIC (FIC Act s.28).

## Scope

1. Forge takes no cash today. Confirm with the sponsor and partner banks whether any channel (cash deposits at a partner, retail cash-in) makes Forge the reporting institution.
2. If cash is ever accepted, the payments orchestrator flags each cash transaction at or above the threshold (R49 999.99 at last reading; verify) and the MLRO files a CTR through goAML within the legal deadline.
