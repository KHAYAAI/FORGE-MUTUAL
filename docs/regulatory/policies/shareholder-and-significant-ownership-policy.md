**Purpose.** Controls who may own Forge and how ownership is recorded.

## Rules

1. The shareholder (or member) register is kept by the company secretary and reconciled to the share registry quarterly.
2. Anyone acquiring more than 15% needs the PA's prior permission (Banks Act s.37); larger thresholds need further approval. Check the current thresholds with legal.
3. Every significant shareholder passes a fit-and-proper review, including source of funds.
4. Mutual bank route: at least seven founders sign the articles; depositor-members vote at general meetings (LIC-07).
