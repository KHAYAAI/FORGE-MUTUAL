**Purpose.** States how much risk Forge is willing to take to meet its plan. Every number is PROPOSED and must be set by the board.

## Statements

1. We hold capital well above the regulatory minimum. PROPOSED: total capital adequacy ratio at least 3 percentage points above the minimum set by the PA; leverage ratio at least 6%.
2. We can survive a severe outflow without help. PROPOSED: LCR at least 130%; NSFR at least 115%; 30 days' survival under the bank's own stress.
3. No one depositor can sink us. PROPOSED: largest depositor at most 10% of deposits; top 20 at most 40% (threshold 1).
4. We lend only what we understand. PROPOSED: credit to tech businesses and their founders only; no unsecured consumer lending beyond overdrafts in year one; non-performing loans below 4%.
5. Crypto: we bank crypto businesses that are licensed by the FSCA as CASPs, and we do not hold crypto assets on our balance sheet until a licence decision says otherwise.
6. Zero tolerance: knowingly dealing with a sanctioned person; tipping off; unreconciled ledger differences older than one business day; customer data outside South Africa without a legal basis.
7. Technology: PROPOSED recovery time of 4 hours for payments and 0 data loss for the ledger.

## Limits and triggers

1. Each statement has a green, amber (early warning) and red (breach) level, reported monthly.
2. A red breach is reported to the board chair and the Risk Committee chair within 1 business day, with a plan.
