**Purpose.** The checklist completed before any provider is used, and repeated yearly for material ones.

## Checklist

1. Legal entity, ownership, sanctions screening of the provider and its owners.
2. Financial standing: last 2 years' audited statements.
3. Security: SOC 2 Type II or ISO 27001 certificate; penetration test summary; breach history.
4. Data: where it is processed and stored, sub-processors, encryption, return and deletion at exit.
5. Resilience: recovery time and point objectives, DR test evidence.
6. Regulatory: the provider will allow the PA and our auditors access; it will notify us of incidents within the time we need to meet our own notice duties (JN-04).
7. Exit: a written exit plan (`exit-plans.md`).

## Contract minimum terms

1. Service levels, audit and access rights for us and the regulator, data location, confidentiality, breach notice, sub-contracting consent, business continuity, termination and exit assistance, no right to cancel because we enter resolution, governing law (OUT-06).
2. Head of Legal reviews every contract before signature.
