**Purpose.** Controls currency risk from USD, EUR and GBP accounts and FX, and interest rate risk in the banking book.

## Currency

1. No proprietary trading. FX is booked against customer deals only; the net open position per currency is closed daily with the partner bank.
2. PROPOSED limit: net open position in all currencies at most 2% of qualifying capital at close of business.
3. Individual customers' annual foreign allowance enforced in the FX service (threshold 28).

## Interest rate in the banking book

1. Measure the change in economic value and net interest income for a 200 basis point shock monthly.
2. PROPOSED limit: economic value change at most 15% of tier 1 capital.

## Governance

1. ALCO reviews monthly; breaches escalate to the CRO the same day.
