**Purpose.** Applies Directive 3 of 2018: governs any use of cloud services and any processing or storing of data outside South Africa.

## Rules

1. Every cloud or offshoring initiative needs a recorded risk-appetite check and due diligence before it starts (D3-03); see `provider-due-diligence-procedure.md`.
2. Data classes marked South Africa-only in `docs/regulatory/data-classes.csv` must not leave South Africa.
3. Material arrangements are notified to the PA in advance (OUT-02).
4. No arrangement may obstruct the PA's access, audit or forensic investigation (D3-05).
5. Every arrangement is in a legally binding agreement (D3-07).

## Default

1. PROPOSED: production in a South African cloud region; ADR-0002 decides the provider.
