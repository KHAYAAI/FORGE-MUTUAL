# Privacy notice (POPIA s.18): customer-facing draft

**What we collect.** Your name, identity number, contact details, and an image of your identity document and a selfie
to verify who you are. For a business: its registration details, directors and owners. Your transactions with us.

**Why.** To open and run your account, to verify your identity and prevent fraud and money laundering (the law requires
it), to meet our reporting duties to regulators, and, only if you agree, to tell you about our products.

**Who else sees it.** Our identity verification partner (Didit), our sign-in provider (WorkOS), our sponsor and partner
banks, and regulators where the law requires. PROPOSED: each named with the country where it processes your data, once
confirmed (POP-08).

**How long.** As set out in our retention schedule: generally five years after you close your account.

**Your rights.** You may ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, object to marketing, and complain to the
Information Regulator. Contact our Information Officer at ________ (to be appointed, POP-01).

**Security.** Identity numbers and contact details are encrypted, and our systems talk to each other only over
authenticated, encrypted connections. If your information is compromised, we will tell you and the Information Regulator.
