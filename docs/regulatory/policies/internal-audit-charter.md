**Purpose.** Sets up internal audit as a permanent, independent function (Banks Act regulation 48).

## Independence

1. Reports functionally to the Audit Committee chair, administratively to the CEO. Owns no controls and no thresholds.
2. Outsourcing to a firm needs the PA's prior written approval and never goes to the external auditor (BR-05).

## Scope

1. Every function and provider, including the code, the CI gate, the ledger and the access review.
2. Yearly: the deposit book, the advances book and large exposures (LIC-14), the threshold register and every change to it, and the AML programme.

## Method

1. A risk-based 3-year plan, approved yearly. IIA standards.
2. Findings rated and tracked to closure; overdue high findings reported to the board.
