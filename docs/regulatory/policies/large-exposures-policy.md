# Large exposures and investment limits policy

Register ids: LIC-14, MR-01. PA structure: PA-03 (credit risk) and PA-09 (investment and exposure limits); see `docs/governance/POLICY_MAP.md`. The PA's
prudential standard on credit risk for mutual banks is announced but not published: re-check this policy against it when it is.

1. **Purpose.** Stop the bank depending on any one counterparty, and keep investments in property, shares, subsidiaries and associates within the
   Mutual Banks Act (sections 55 and 56).
2. **What an exposure is.** The total owed by, placed with or committed to one legal entity and its connected persons: deposits placed with it,
   loans and advances, investments, acknowledgements of debt, contingent liabilities and 10% of gross forward exchange contracts (DI 510
   instructions, Annexure Q2). **Balances with the sponsor bank and partner banks are exposures to those banks.**
3. **Limits.** PROPOSED: no single exposure above 25% of net qualifying capital (DI 100 line 31) without board approval, and none above the limit
   regulation 30 sets (VERIFY the current figure). Placements with banks are spread so that no bank holds more than the board sets; the plan to
   reach that is in the business plan (concentration on the sponsor).
4. **Reporting.** Every exposure above 10% of capital goes on DI 510 each quarter; a transaction that takes an exposure above the prescribed
   percentage is reported on DI 505 when it happens (`services/bank/reg-reporting/internal/returns/di510.go`). Investments under sections 55 and 56
   are reported yearly on DI 700, and a breach qualifies the DI 099 declaration.
5. **Approval.** A new exposure above 10% of capital needs the credit committee; above 25%, the board. The person proposing it cannot approve it.
6. **Review.** Yearly by the board risk committee, and when the PA publishes its credit-risk standard.
