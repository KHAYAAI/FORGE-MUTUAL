# Outsourcing and third-party policy

Purpose: control use of providers, including cloud and offshore processing (Directive 3/2018; Guidance Note 5/2014; Joint Standard 2 of 2024). Register ids: D3-01 to D3-06, OUT-01 to OUT-05.

## Rules
1. **Materiality.** Before a provider is used, assess whether the service is material: would failure or poor performance threaten customers, safety and soundness, or our ability to meet obligations? Record the assessment in the supplier register below.
2. **Approval.** Material outsourcing needs board approval and, where the guidance requires, notice to the Prudential Authority before it starts. Offshoring and cloud use for material functions: obtain PA approval or no-objection in line with Directive 3/2018 (legal to confirm the exact route).
3. **Due diligence.** Financial standing, security posture (independent reports), data location, sub-contractors, exit.
4. **Contract.** Must give: audit and access rights for us and the regulator; data location and return; security and breach notice duties; sub-contracting limits; business continuity; termination and exit assistance; compliance with law.
5. **Data.** No personal data is sent offshore without a recorded legal basis and the controls above. PROPOSED default: South African region.
6. **Monitoring.** Review each material provider yearly; keep an exit plan for each.
