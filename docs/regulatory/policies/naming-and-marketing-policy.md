**Purpose.** Keeps public communication honest and lawful before and after licensing.

## Rules

1. Until authorised by the PA we do not call ourselves a bank in a business name or advert (LIC-17). The front door already states that Forge is not yet a licensed bank.
2. Every product advert shows costs and risks clearly and is approved by compliance.
3. Crypto-related communication states that crypto assets are not deposits and are not covered by deposit insurance.
