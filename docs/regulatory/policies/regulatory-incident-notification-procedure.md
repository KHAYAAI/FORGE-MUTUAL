**Purpose.** Who is told, by when, after a material cyber incident, a data breach or an operational outage.

## Classification

1. The incident manager classifies within 1 hour of detection using `incident-response-plan.md`.
2. Material: customer funds or data at risk, critical services down beyond the recovery time, or regulator attention likely.

## Notifications

1. Prudential Authority and FSCA: within the time set in Joint Notice 2 of 2026 and its template (JS2-18). Confirm the exact hours with legal; do not rely on a provider's notice (JN-04).
2. Information Regulator and affected people: as soon as reasonably possible after a personal data compromise (POPIA s.22).
3. Sponsor bank: as its contract requires.
4. The board chair and Risk Committee chair: same day.

## Records

1. Every notification, its time and its content are kept in the incident record.
