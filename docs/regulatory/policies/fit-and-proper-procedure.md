# Fit-and-proper procedure

Register ids: LIC-05, LIC-06. The legal test and the PA's forms were not read in full for this draft; counsel must confirm them. This is a procedure for gathering and keeping the information, not an assessment of anyone.

For each director, executive officer, significant shareholder and controller, before appointment and then at least yearly:
1. Identity and residence verification.
2. Qualifications and experience relevant to the role.
3. Declaration of directorships, interests and related-person dealings.
4. Declaration of criminal, regulatory, insolvency and litigation history; checks with the person's consent (criminal record, credit, sanctions, adverse media).
5. Source of funds for shareholders.
6. A written assessment by a person other than the candidate, approved by the board (or, before a board exists, recorded for the PA).
7. Records kept, access restricted (Restricted class), and changes reported to the PA as required.

Founders and prospective directors are not yet assessed. Do not store this information in the product's databases until its handling is approved.
