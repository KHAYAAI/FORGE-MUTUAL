# Staff, contractor and provider screening (Joint Standard 1 of 2023; FICA s.42A)

Register ids: JS1-06, FIC-10.

Before access to any system or customer data (PROPOSED): identity verification; criminal record check; credit check
for roles handling money or credit decisions; qualification and reference checks; a signed confidentiality agreement
and acceptable-use policy. Key persons (directors, executive officers, MLRO, CRO, heads of control functions) also go
through `fit-and-proper-procedure.md`. Repeat every 3 years, or on a change of role. Providers' staff with access to
Forge data are screened by the provider under contract, which must say so (`outsourcing-register.csv`). Access is
removed on the last working day; the quarterly access review (`../access-review.md`) confirms it.
