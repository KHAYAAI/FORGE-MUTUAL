**Purpose.** Gives every committee in `docs/governance/STRUCTURE.md` its mandate, membership, quorum and reporting line.

## Audit committee

1. Chair: independent NED, CA(SA) (R29). Members: 3 independent NEDs. Quarterly.
2. Oversees financial statements, regulatory returns, internal control over financial reporting and the external auditors.
3. Appoints, directs and evaluates internal audit; approves the audit plan; receives every audit report (BR-05).
4. Meets internal and external audit without management each quarter.
5. Quorum: 2 members.

## Risk and capital management committee

1. Chair: independent NED (R30). Members: 3 NEDs; CEO, CFO, CRO attend. Quarterly.
2. Recommends the risk appetite and the risk management framework; approves risk policies delegated by the board.
3. Reviews ICAAP, ILAAP, stress tests, the recovery plan and the risk register.
4. Receives the CRO, MLRO and CISO reports directly.
5. Quorum: 2 NEDs.

## IT and cyber committee

1. Chair: independent NED, technology (R31). Quarterly.
2. Approves the IT strategy, cyber strategy and the IT risk framework (JS1, JS2).
3. Reviews penetration tests, incidents, DR tests and material IT outsourcing.

## Remuneration committee

1. Chair: independent NED (R32). Twice a year.
2. Sets pay for executives; control-function pay is not tied to the results of the business they oversee; deferral and clawback for material risk takers.

## Directors' affairs and nominations committee

1. Chair: board chair. Twice a year.
2. Board composition, skills matrix, succession, fit and proper, independence and evaluation.

## Social and ethics committee

1. Companies Act s.72 and Regulation 43 duties: ethics, customer fairness, consumer relationships, labour and environment.

## Board credit committee

1. Chair: independent NED. Monthly once credit is live.
2. Approves exposures above the management Credit Committee's mandate and every large exposure (PROPOSED: above 10% of qualifying capital).

## Executive committee

1. Chair: CEO Tebogo Mvelase. Weekly. Runs the bank within the appetite and the budget; escalates breaches to the board.

## ALCO

1. Chair: CFO. Members: CEO, CRO, Head of Treasury. Monthly, and daily during a liquidity stress.
2. Owns the liquidity and contingency funding plan, interest rate risk in the banking book, FX positions, deposit and loan pricing.

## Credit committee

1. Chair: Head of Credit. Weekly. Decides within the mandate in `credit-risk-policy.md`; reviews arrears, notices, restructures and write-offs.

## Operational risk and control committee

1. Chair: CRO. Monthly. Incidents, losses, control failures, the risk register, key risk indicators.

## Financial crime committee

1. Chair: MLRO. Monthly. Monitoring rule changes, case backlog against SLA (thresholds 4 and 5), customer exits, sanctions, STR statistics (no customer details in minutes).

## Product approval committee

1. Chair: Head of Product. CRO and CCO must both agree. Approves new products and material changes; issues the `DEC-nnnn` decision that turns a product on through `FORGE_APPROVAL_<PRODUCT>`.

## Change advisory board

1. Chair: CTO. Weekly. Approves production changes; reviews emergency changes after the fact; no change without a second reviewer in code.

## Data governance council

1. Chair: Head of Data. Quarterly. Owns `docs/regulatory/data-classes.csv`, retention and residency.
