**Purpose.** The standard of behaviour expected of everyone who works for or on behalf of Forge.

## We

1. Act honestly and treat customers fairly.
2. Keep customer information confidential and use it only for the purpose it was given.
3. Never tip off a customer about a report or investigation.
4. Report concerns early, including through the whistleblowing channel.
5. Do not trade on inside information or use our position for personal gain.

## Breaches

1. Handled through the disciplinary process; serious breaches reported to the board and, where required, to regulators.
