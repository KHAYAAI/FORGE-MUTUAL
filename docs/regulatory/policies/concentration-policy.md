# Deposit concentration and liquidity limits policy

Register ids: BR-04 (related). Not drafted from the regulation text: the Banks Act regulations on liquidity and large exposures were not read in full. Thresholds below are placeholders consistent with `docs/THRESHOLDS.md`, not measured limits.

1. **Purpose.** Prevent dependence on a few depositors or one funding source.
2. **Measures.** Share of total deposits held by the largest 1, 5 and 10 depositors; share of funding from one sector; net outflow over 1 and 7 days; liquid assets as a share of deposits.
3. **Limits.** PROPOSED: set by the board once a deposit base exists. The treasury-risk service raises alerts when configured thresholds are crossed; owners are named in `docs/THRESHOLD_OWNERS.md` (not yet named).
4. **Response.** An alert goes to the operations lead, who records a decision (slow payouts, seek funding, tell the sponsor bank). Repeated breaches go to the board.
5. **Reporting.** Monthly to the board; prudential liquidity returns when licensed (form definitions not yet read).
