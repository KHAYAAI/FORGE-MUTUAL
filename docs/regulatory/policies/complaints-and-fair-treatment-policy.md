**Purpose.** Delivers the six Treating Customers Fairly outcomes and a complaints process that meets the Ombudsman for Banking Services' standard.

## Complaints

1. Acknowledged within 1 business day; resolved within 15 business days (PROPOSED); escalation to the Ombudsman for Banking Services explained in every final reply.
2. Root causes reviewed monthly by the executive committee.

## Fair treatment

1. Products designed for an identified customer group and approved by the product approval committee.
2. Clear terms, no hidden fees; vulnerable customers identified and supported.
3. Declined applicants are told the outcome, never a financial-crime reason (no tipping off).
