**Purpose.** Keeps capital above the minimum at all times, plans it three years ahead and tests it under stress (internal capital adequacy assessment process).

## Minimum capital

1. Bank: R250 million initial capital; mutual bank: R10 million (LIC-02). Written proof is part of the application.
2. Capital adequacy ratio and leverage ratio calculated daily by the prudential module; buffers per the risk appetite.

## ICAAP

1. Yearly: identify all material risks, quantify capital for each, run at least three stress scenarios (severe recession, rand shock, largest provider failure), and set a capital plan.
2. Approved by the board and submitted to the PA when required.

## Capital actions

1. Dividends only if the plan stays within the appetite after the stress test. Capital raises planned 12 months ahead.
