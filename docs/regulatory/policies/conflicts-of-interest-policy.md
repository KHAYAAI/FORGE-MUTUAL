# Conflicts of interest and related-party policy

| | |

**Purpose.** Stops personal interests of directors, staff and shareholders from influencing the bank's decisions.

## Rules

1. Every director and employee declares interests on joining, yearly, and when anything changes. The company secretary keeps the register.
2. A conflicted person leaves the discussion and does not vote.
3. Loans and services to directors, executives, significant shareholders and their related parties are on normal terms, approved by the Board Credit Committee, and reported to the board.
4. Gifts above R1 000 (PROPOSED) are declared; above R5 000 are refused.
