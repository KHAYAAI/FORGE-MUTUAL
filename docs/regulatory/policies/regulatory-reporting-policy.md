**Purpose.** Governs how BA (bank) or DI (mutual bank) returns are built, checked, certified and submitted.

## Inventory

1. Bank route: BA 100 to BA 950. Mutual bank route: DI 100, 110, 200, 300, 310, 400 to 430, 500 to 520, 600, 700 and others (MR-06).
2. Each return has an owner, a due date, a source in the ledger, and an automated builder (none built yet: engineering item).

## Process

1. Built from the ledger by code; reconciled to the trial balance; reviewed; certified by the CFO and, where required, a director; audited where the regulations require.
2. Late or wrong returns are reported to the Audit Committee and the PA.
