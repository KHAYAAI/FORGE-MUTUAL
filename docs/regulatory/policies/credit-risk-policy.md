**Purpose.** Governs lending: overdrafts, working-capital loans and revenue-based financing in `services/bank/lending-service`.

## Who may lend

1. Only products approved through the product approval committee and switched on by a `DEC-nnnn` decision.
2. PROPOSED mandates: credit officer up to R250 000 per customer; Credit Committee up to R2 000 000; Board Credit Committee above that and for every large exposure.

## Assessment

1. Affordability under the National Credit Act where it applies; for juristic persons, cash-flow based on 6 months of account data.
2. A credit bureau check before every facility (bureau not yet chosen).
3. Pre-agreement statement and quotation before the customer signs (NCA).

## Pricing

1. Rates and fees within the NCA maximums; placeholders are thresholds 17 to 21.

## Monitoring and arrears

1. Daily days-past-due; monthly statements; default notice at 20 days (threshold 27) before any enforcement.
2. Restructures and write-offs decided by the Credit Committee; IFRS 9 staging reviewed monthly by the CFO.

## Concentration

1. Sector and single-name limits in `concentration-policy.md`.
