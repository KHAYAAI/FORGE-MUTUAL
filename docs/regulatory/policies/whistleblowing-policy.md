**Purpose.** Gives staff, contractors and providers a safe way to report wrongdoing (Protected Disclosures Act 26 of 2000).

## Channels

1. An independent, anonymous hotline run by a third party (to be chosen).
2. Direct to the Audit Committee chair for reports about executives.

## Protection

1. No one suffers for a disclosure made in good faith. Retaliation is a disciplinary offence.

## Handling

1. Acknowledged within 2 business days; investigated independently; outcome reported to the Audit Committee quarterly.
