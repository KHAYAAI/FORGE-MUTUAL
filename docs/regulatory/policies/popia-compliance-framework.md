**Purpose.** How Forge meets the Protection of Personal Information Act and the Promotion of Access to Information Act.

## Officers

1. Information Officer: Tebogo Mvelase as head of the body, until the board delegates. Must be registered with the Information Regulator before processing customer data (POP-01).
2. Deputy Information Officer: VACANT (proposed: Head of Data, R23).

## Framework

1. Personal information impact assessment for each product and provider.
2. Data subject requests answered within 30 days; the `GET /v1/my-data` endpoint supports access requests.
3. Transborder flows only to adequate jurisdictions, with consent or under a binding contract (POP-08); each provider's processing location is recorded in the outsourcing register.
4. Privacy notice: `privacy-notice.md`; retention: `data-retention-schedule.md`.
