**Purpose.** Pay that rewards sound, long-term performance and does not encourage excessive risk.

## Principles

1. Variable pay for material risk takers is partly deferred (PROPOSED: 40% over 3 years) and subject to malus and clawback.
2. Control functions (risk, compliance, MLRO, internal audit) are paid on their own objectives, not the results of the business they oversee.
3. No guaranteed bonuses beyond the first year of employment.
4. Disclosure in the annual report as the regulations require.
