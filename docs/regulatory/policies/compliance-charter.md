**Purpose.** Establishes an independent compliance function that manages regulatory risk across every law that applies to Forge.

## Independence

1. The CCO reports to the CEO for administration and to the Audit Committee directly; may not be dismissed without board approval.
2. Unrestricted access to people, records and systems.

## Duties

1. Keep the regulatory universe: `docs/regulatory/register.csv` (114 requirements today) plus laws not yet read (GAP rows).
2. A risk-based compliance monitoring plan approved yearly by the Audit Committee.
3. Advise on new products, providers and changes; sign off with the CRO in the product approval committee.
4. Train staff; report breaches to regulators where the law requires.
5. Own the relationship with the PA, FSCA, NCR and Information Regulator jointly with the CEO.

## Reporting

1. Quarterly to the Audit Committee: open register rows, breaches, regulator correspondence, monitoring results.
