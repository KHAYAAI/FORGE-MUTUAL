**Purpose.** Covers how Forge learns about threats and proves its controls work (Joint Standard 2 of 2024; Guidance Note 4 of 2017).

## Threat intelligence

1. Subscribe to SABRIC alerts, the national CSIRT and vendor advisories; join an industry information-sharing group (JS2-15).
2. Weekly triage by the CISO; relevant threats become risk register entries or changes.

## Testing

1. Dependency and code scanning on every change (in the CI gate today).
2. Quarterly vulnerability scans of every internet-facing system.
3. A yearly penetration test of internet-facing critical systems by a reputable independent firm, and after any major change (JS2-16).
4. Every finding goes in a remediation register with an owner and a due date: critical 7 days, high 30, medium 90 (PROPOSED).

## Self-assessment

1. Yearly assessment of our policies against the CPMI-IOSCO cyber-resilience guidance (G4-01).
