**Purpose.** No product reaches customers without risk, compliance, finance, operations and technology sign-off.

## Steps

1. Product proposal: customer, need, economics, risks, regulatory basis.
2. Assessments: CRO (risk and appetite), CCO (law and conduct), MLRO (financial crime), CFO (capital, liquidity, accounting), COO (operations), CTO and CISO (technology and security), Information Officer (privacy).
3. Decision by the product approval committee, recorded as `DEC-nnnn` in `docs/DECISIONS.md`; credit and deposit products also go to the Risk Committee.
4. Production switch: `FORGE_APPROVAL_<PRODUCT>=DEC-nnnn` (enforced by `pkg/licence`).
5. Post-launch review after 6 months.
