**Purpose.** Manages losses from failed processes, people, systems and external events, including fraud.

## Tools

1. Risk and control self-assessment per function, twice a year.
2. Loss and near-miss register: every incident over R10 000 (PROPOSED) or any customer impact is logged within 2 business days.
3. Key risk indicators: suspense balance (threshold 3), reconciliation age (threshold 12), failed payouts, case backlog.
4. Scenario analysis yearly for low-frequency, high-impact events.

## Controls

1. Maker-checker on every manual money movement and every threshold change.
2. Segregation of duties enforced in code (caller allow-lists, roles) and reviewed in `docs/regulatory/access-review.md`.

## Fraud

1. Fraud lead (R24) owns detection rules; confirmed fraud is reported to SABRIC and, where required, to SAPS and the FIC.
