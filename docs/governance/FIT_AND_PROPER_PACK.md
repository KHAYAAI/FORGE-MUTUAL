## What each person provides

1. The completed and signed DI 020 or BA 020 statement.
2. Certified copy of identity document or passport.
3. A full CV: every role in the last 10 years, with reasons for leaving.
4. Certified copies of qualifications, and professional registrations (for example SAICA, CFA, IIA).
5. Police clearance certificate from SAPS, and from every country lived in for more than a year in the last 10 years.
6. Credit report from a registered bureau, with an explanation of any judgment, default or debt review.
7. Declarations: insolvency, disqualification as a director (Companies Act s.69), regulatory sanctions, dismissals, civil or criminal proceedings, and investigations.
8. Two references from people who have supervised or worked closely with them in a regulated role.
9. Directorships and interests held now, for the conflicts register (`conflicts-of-interest-policy.md`).
10. For significant shareholders or founding members: source of funds and source of wealth.

## What Forge checks before filing

1. Identity verified (the same Didit check customers go through).
2. Sanctions and PEP screening (`pkg/screening`), repeated yearly.
3. Qualifications and professional registrations confirmed with the issuer.
4. References taken by the Company Secretary or Nominations Committee chair, not by the candidate's future line manager.
5. Skills against the seat's requirements in `ROLES.md`, recorded by the Nominations Committee.
6. Time commitment: other roles leave enough time for this one.
