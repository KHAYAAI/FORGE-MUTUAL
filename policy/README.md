# Policy bundle

Rego rules (`forge/<package>/`) and data (`*.json` at this level, each file becomes `data.<name>`)
make up the bundle. `manifest.json` and `manifest.sig` are generated by `cmd/policy-sign` and must
match the files exactly. Test files (`*_test.rego`) are not part of the signed bundle.

Figures in `tiers.json` and the sector list in `sectors.json` are development placeholders. They must
be confirmed by the risk owner and by the ADR-0012 decision before any real customer is onboarded.
